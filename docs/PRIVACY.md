# Privacy Policy: Hunting with Zack

_Draft. Last updated: [date]. Publish this at a public URL (for example GitHub Pages) before submitting to the stores._

**Who we are.** Hunting with Zack is built by [Trent / StrategicSystems], [contact email].

**What the app stores on your phone**
- Your hunt log: trips, species sightings with times, and optional notes.
- Settings, such as species, WMU, hunter, style and wind.
- Cached weather forecasts.

This data stays on your device unless you turn on sync.

**Location**
- The app asks for your location only when you use features that need it: finding the WMU you're in, getting sun times for your position, or tagging a sighting with where it happened.
- Location is used on your device. It's saved with a sighting only if you turn that on.
- The app never tracks your location in the background.

**Data sent off your phone**
- **Weather:** the app sends the coordinates of a nearby town (or your position, if you allow it) to our weather provider, [Open-Meteo / Environment and Climate Change Canada], to get a forecast. No account or personal identifier is sent.
- **Sync (optional, only if you join a hunting party):** your trips and sightings are sent over an encrypted connection to our server so your party members can see them. Sighting locations are synced only if you turn that on.

**What we don't do.** There are no ads and no analytics. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't track you across apps.

**Your choices.** You can export your hunt log, or delete it in the app. If you use sync, you can delete your account and all synced data from Settings → Account, or by emailing [contact email]. We remove server data within 30 days.

**Children.** This app is meant for adult hunters and isn't directed at children under 13.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
